## About sciwork

sciwork is a community of researchers and engineers to share and discuss computer code for scientific, numerical, and engineering applications. We believe in the power of openness and use open source as a means to advance software development in computational sciences.

## About sciwork conference 2025

In 2025, we will hold a physical conference, sciwork conference 2025, to bring together enthusiasts in science, engineering, and programming to share and exchange ideas. The event will cover topics including scientific computing, high-performance computing, data management, analytics, and visualization.

During the event, participants will have the opportunity to engage closely with professionals through project introductions, technical discussions, and training sessions, gaining insights into the latest developments in scientific computing, engineering, and open-source software.

## Why sponsor sciwork conference 2025?

By sponsoring sciwork conference 2025, your organization will:

- Connect directly with top researchers, engineers, and open-source contributors in Taiwan’s scientific computing community
- Showcase your brand among professionals working in fields such as AI, HPC, data science, and software engineering
- Support open science and technology education through hands-on learning and collaboration
- Gain visibility through onsite and digital promotion channels before, during, and after the event

We welcome organizations that share our passion for open science and technology. Your support will help us:

- Provide accessible technical workshops and community events
- Invite expert speakers and educators
- Grow the local open-source ecosystem

## Audience profile

- Professionals and researchers in scientific computing, engineering, and data science
- Developers and contributors to open-source projects
- Students and early-career professionals seeking technical mentorship
- Academia and industry experts collaborating on computational research
