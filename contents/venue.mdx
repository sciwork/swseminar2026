## By Car:

Navigate to the EC Building, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University: [Google Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XDEJry5h155iCzEt8)

**Parking Information**

For on-campus parking, you may use the [paid parking facilities](https://www.nycu.edu.tw/userfiles/gach/files/20240112172629456.pdf) near the auditorium or the tennis field.

## THSR:

Upon arriving at Hsinchu THSR station, there are two bus lines leading to the venue:

**By Bus:**

1. Take Pilot Bus (先導公車) toward Bei-Da Bridge (北大橋) to Bamboo lake (竹湖) stop and walk 5min.
2. Take Bus 182 toward Bei-Da Bridge (北大橋) to NYCU Guangfu Road (陽明交大光復路) stop and walk 20min.

**By Taxi:**

- A taxi ride from Hsinchu THSR station to the venue costs approximately 230-280 NTD.
