# Call for Proposals

The sciwork seminar 2026 brings together professionals, researchers,
and enthusiasts in the scientific computing, data and open-source community.
This year's seminar will be held as a **physical event**.

We are warmly inviting you to submit proposals for talks, covering a wide
range of topics related to the conference theme.

## Conference Topics (including but not limited to):

1. Aerospace & Aviation Technologies

2. AI / Machine Learning, LLM / Vector Embeddings, MLOps

3. AI Application / Automation, Agentive AI

4. CAD / CAE / CAM

5. Image Processing & Computer Vision

6. FinTech, Quantitative Trading

7. Blockchain, Web3 / Data Infrastructure

8. Robotics, Autonomous Systems, Drones

9. Edge Computing

10. XR / AR / VR / MR

11. Embedded Systems

12. DevOps, CI/CD, Observability, Reproducible Research

13. Security / Cybersecurity, Privacy, Safety

14. Defense Technology

15. Quantum Technologies

16. Semiconductor Trends (e.g., silicon photonics)

17. Clean Energy / Green Computing

18. Other scientific computing & open-source case studies

## Submission Guidelines:

1. Presentation types: Regular presentation (30 mins talk, including Q & A)
2. Send your proposal to [cfp@sciwork.dev](mailto:cfp@sciwork.dev)

- **Title**: Provide a clear and concise title for your talk.
- **Abstract**: Write a brief abstract summarizing the content of your talk.
- **Description**: Provide additional information.

We aim to be an accessible, community-driven conference, with novice to
advanced level presentations. If you have an innovative idea, project,
or case study to share with our community, we would be delighted to hear
from you how to solve problems and grow a stronger open-source community
together.

If you have any questions or need assistance with your proposal, please
do not hesitate to contact us at **contact@sciwork.dev**.

We look forward to receiving your submissions and your participation in
the sciwork seminar 2026.
